Don't let Hapell's muteness and lighthearted nature fool you! She is a forward-thinking, tough-minded individual who is adamant on what she wants, and that is to help people out. If something doesn't make sense to someone, she is willing to explain it to them no matter how long it takes, even if it gets tiring. Hapell's lifelong love of deconstructing things around the house led her to become an audio specialist. Besides tinkering with weird machinery, she often hangs upside down from her bed, cooks the weirdest looking meals and keeps a collection of 8-tracks.
